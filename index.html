      About

      A browser darkroom modeled on LightCraft’s develop module. Raw files open through their embedded preview.

      Everything in Safelight is free. AI features run on this device with open models: LaMa (Apache 2.0) and MI-GAN (MIT) for Remove, BiRefNet (MIT) for Select subject, SegFormer (NVIDIA, non-commercial license) for Select sky, SlimSAM (Apache 2.0) for Select object, Depth Anything V2 Small and Depth Anything 3 Mono Large (Apache 2.0) for Lens blur, Real-ESRGAN (BSD 3-Clause) for Upscale, NAFNet (MIT) for AI Denoise and Fix motion blur, and DDColor (Apache 2.0) for Colorize. Each model downloads once, the first time you use it. Generative edit, Relight and Generative expand use either Qwen’s free demo Space on Hugging Face, within your account’s daily GPU minutes, or FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache 2.0) on your own Mac. Ask uses Claude on claude.ai and Qwen3-VL (Apache 2.0) through Ollama on your Mac elsewhere. Fix faces uses GFPGAN (Apache 2.0) inside IOPaint on your Mac.
